Membership & Sign-Ups

We welcome new members at any time during the school year. Whether you want to compete at major collegiate conferences or develop your public speaking and debate skills, there is a place for you at JFK Model UN. To join at any time, simply attend any of the meetings and speak to an officer for more information.
