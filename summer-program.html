2026 Summer Program - Completed

Our 2026 summer program was a great success! Over the course of six weeks, middle school students learned the fundamentals of Model United Nations, including research techniques, resolution drafting, and public speaking skills.


Program Summary

  • Dates: Tuesdays, June 16 – July 21, 2026
  • Time: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Location: Millard Elementary School
  • Participants: 40+ middle school students
