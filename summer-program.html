Summer Program (2026 Recap)

Our 2026 summer program was a phenomenal success! Over the course of six weeks, incoming middle school students learned the fundamentals of Model United Nations, including research techniques and writing effective resolutions; in addition, they learned to present themselves confidently and think on the debate floor.

Program Overview

  • Schedule: Tuesdays, June 16 – July 21, 2026
  • Time: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Location: Millard Elementary School
  • Delegates: 40+ middle school participants
